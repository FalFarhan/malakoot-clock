Adhan and alert recordings for ملكوت (Earth Clock)

  albukhait.mp3   Default adhan voice — أذان نجدي، محمد البخيت.
  binmajid.mp3    Second adhan voice — أذان بن ماجد، يوسف الجهني.
  default.mp3     Alias of the default voice (currently albukhait.mp3).
  alarm.mp3       Simple / pre-prayer alert (the short digital alarm).

Also copy the same default voice over  ../adhan.mp3  (legacy path).

The in-app "Adhan voice" setting chooses albukhait or binmajid. Prayer-time alerts
play the selected adhan; the "N minutes before" reminder and the "Simple alert"
option play alarm.mp3.

Formats: MP3, mono, ~96–128 kbps, a few MB each. After replacing a file, rebuild
the site and (for the phone app) a new Android AAB.
